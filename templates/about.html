About Diabetes

Diabetes is a chronic disease that affects how your body converts food into energy. Early diagnosis and a healthy lifestyle can help reduce complications.

What is Diabetes?

Diabetes occurs when the body cannot produce enough insulin or cannot use insulin effectively, causing high blood sugar levels.

Common Symptoms

  • Frequent urination
  • Excessive thirst
  • Fatigue
  • Blurred vision
  • Slow wound healing

Risk Factors

  • Obesity
  • Family History
  • High Blood Pressure
  • Physical Inactivity
  • Unhealthy Diet

Prevention

  • Eat a balanced diet
  • Exercise regularly
  • Maintain healthy weight
  • Monitor blood sugar
  • Sleep 7–9 hours
